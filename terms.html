Solve times, sessions and settings are stored in your browser, not on our servers. We cannot recover them for you. Clearing your browser data deletes them permanently, and so, sometimes, does a browser deciding on its own to reclaim storage.

If a session matters to you, export it. We mean this sincerely: it is the single most common way people lose data on sites built like this one, and it is entirely preventable.

Account details, friends, Battle rooms and AI Coach sessions are stored on our side. The Privacy Policy sets out exactly what, why, and for how long.

7. The AI Coach

The Coach is a training aid built on a large language model. Treat it the way you would treat advice from a stranger at a competition who has looked at your times: often useful, sometimes wrong, never authoritative.

  • Its output is not professional coaching, medical advice, or physiotherapy. If your hands or wrists hurt, see a doctor, not a chatbot.
  • It can be confidently wrong. Do not follow a plan that hurts, and do not treat its numbers as measurements.
  • To produce an answer we send your solve statistics to an AI provider. If you upload a video, the video goes to Google to be analysed. Both are described in the Privacy Policy.

By uploading a video you confirm that you have the right to do so, that it is of you, and that anyone else who appears in it has agreed. Do not upload footage of other people — especially other people's children — without their permission. We delete the file after it has been analysed.
