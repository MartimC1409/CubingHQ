Contact

CubingHQ is run by one person, so messages go straight to the person who wrote the code. Expect a reply within a few days.

Email

martimcbusiness@gmail.com

Email is the most reliable way to reach us, and the right channel for anything urgent or anything involving your personal data.

What to get in touch about

  • Bugs. Tell us your browser and device, and what you did just before it went wrong. A screenshot helps more than a description.
  • Wrong algorithms. If a case in the algorithm database is wrong or has a better-known alternative, say which set and which case.
  • Scramble or ruling problems. If a simulated round scored something in a way that does not match the WCA Regulations, we want to know — include the event and format.
  • Reporting a Battle room. If someone posted something that breaks the Terms of Use, send the room name and roughly when you saw it.
  • Copyright. If material here is yours and should not be, tell us and we will remove it promptly.
  • Privacy requests. Access, correction or deletion of anything we hold — see the Privacy Policy. We action these within 30 days and you do not need to give a reason.
  • Feature ideas. Genuinely welcome. Most of what is here started as someone's suggestion.

Send a message

The form stores your message so we can read it later; it does not open your mail client. What you type here is covered by the Privacy Policy. If you would rather not use it, plain email works just as well.
